# Executive & Board Risk Committee Reporting
**Governance Cadence:** Monthly Operational Risk & Resilience Committee (`ORRC`) · Quarterly Board Risk Committee (`BRC`) · Annual Statutory CBI Filings (`ICT-SAT`, `DORA RoI`, `TIBER-IE`)

---

## 1. Purpose: Translating Operational Telemetry Into Board Assurance
Operational registers across Pillars 1–6 contain hundreds of technical records—vulnerabilities, failover timings, vendor LEIs, incident timestamps, and AI FRIAs. The **`executive-reporting/`** layer distills that granular telemetry into concise, decision-ready reporting so **Independent Non-Executive Directors (`INEDs`)** and **Pre-Approval Controlled Function (`PCF`)** holders can:
1. Verify at a glance whether the Payment Institution is operating inside its **Board-approved Risk Appetite**.
2. Discharge their personal **CBI Individual Accountability Framework (`IAF/SEAR`)** "Reasonable Steps" obligations with documented evidence packs.
3. Approve formal governance actions—including annual `CIBS` Impact Tolerances, `90-day+` Risk Exceptions, TIBER-IE TLPT closures, and annual **EBA DPM 4.0 DORA Register of Information** submissions.

---

## 2. Executive Reporting Cadence

| Reporting Forum & Artefact | Frequency | Audience & Sign-Off | Core Contents & Decision Gates |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly Security & Resilience Flash** | Weekly | `PCF-46` (CTO), `PCF-52` (MLRO), `PCF-14` (CRO) | Critical/KEV patch SLA status, `45m` incident triage metrics, unauthorized script blocks, and Shadow AI proxy blocks. |
| **Operational Risk & Resilience Committee (`ORRC`) Pack** | Monthly | Executive Committee (`PCF-1`, `PCF-14`, `PCF-46`, `PCF-52`, `PCF-56`) | Monthly minor incident aggregation (`DORA Art. 8(2)`), `CAPA` closure tracking, `< 90-day` risk exceptions, and vendor DDQ turnaround. |
| **[Quarterly Board Risk Committee (`BRC`) Pack](q4-2026-board-risk-committee-pack.md)** | Quarterly | Board Risk Committee (`INEDs`) & Statutory Auditors | Consolidated **Enterprise KRI Scorecard**, Pillar 1–6 RAG posture, quarterly BCDR failover results, quarterly AI vendor audit assurance, and `IAF/SEAR` PCF attestations. |
| **Annual Supervisory & Board Charter Pack** | Annual | Full Board & Central Bank of Ireland (`CBI`) | Annual `CIBS` Impact Tolerance & harm calibration approval, `ICT-SAT` maturity self-assessment, `DORA RoI` XBRL sign-off, and annual `Art. 4` AI literacy report. |
